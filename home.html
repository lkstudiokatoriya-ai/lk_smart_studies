Help & FAQ

Common questions about LK SMART STUDIES

How can I find study material?
You can find all study materials in the Student section on the dashboard. Simply select your subject to view available PDFs, notes, and resources. You can also use the main search bar at the top of the page.
Where are the PDF notes located?
PDF notes are organized under the 'PDF Notes' category within the Student Zone. They are categorized by subject and chapter for easy access and reading.
What is the Family section?
The Family section is a dedicated space for parents and guardians. It provides tips, communication strategies, and advice on how to support students in establishing a healthy study routine at home.
How will PDF uploads work?
Currently, the upload feature in the Teacher Corner is a visual placeholder. In the future, authorized educators will be able to select a file from their device and upload it directly to the platform's database to share with students.
