General FAQ

What is LK SMART STUDIES?
LK SMART STUDIES is a premium digital learning platform designed to provide structured study resources, PDF notes, previous year question papers, and educational updates for students across various academic levels.
What can I find on LK SMART STUDIES?
You can find organized PDF notes, PYQs (Previous Year Questions), model papers, scholarship updates, admission notices, and guidance for students, families, and teachers.
Is LK SMART STUDIES a website or an app?
It is a highly responsive web application that works seamlessly on both mobile browsers and desktop computers. It can also be installed as a PWA (Progressive Web App) on your device.
How can I start using the website?
Simply navigate to the Home page or Student Hub, select your educational goal (like School or Polytechnic), and browse the structured subjects and resources.

Student FAQ

How do I choose my study goal?
Go to the Student Hub page. The first section will ask you to choose between School, Polytechnic/Diploma, B.Tech, or Other Exams. Click your goal to proceed.
How do I select School, Board, and Class?
Once you click "School" on the Student Hub, a step-by-step interface will appear. First, choose your Board (e.g., CBSE, BSEB), then select your Class, and finally pick a Subject to see available resources.
How do I find Polytechnic or B.Tech resources?
Select the respective card on the Student Hub. You will then be guided to select your State/System, followed by your admission type, semester/year, and finally your branch.
How does Exam Preparation work?
The Exam Preparation section provides structured guidance, motivation, and study strategies to help you efficiently complete your syllabus and manage exam pressure.

Family FAQ

What is the Family section?
It is a dedicated hub for parents and guardians. It provides educational advice on how to support a student's learning journey at home, beyond the classroom.
How can parents support a student?
Parents can support students by encouraging consistent effort, helping organize study materials, creating a quiet environment, and listening without judgment.
How can parents support exam preparation?
By respecting study time, reducing unnecessary pressure, keeping communication positive, and helping ensure they have all necessary tools ready before exam day.

Teacher FAQ

What is the Teacher section?
The Teacher Workspace is designed for educators to upload PDFs, write educational content, publish announcements, and manage their shared resources.
Why is Teacher Upload currently unavailable?
The current Teacher Workspace is a frontend UI preview. The features are visually complete, but actions like file uploading, publishing, and real-time database management are pending backend integration.
When will the real Teacher system be connected?
Authentication, secure file storage, and Airtable/Database connections will be implemented in a future update by the development team.

PDF & Notes FAQ

Where will PDF Notes be available?
PDF notes are available in the Student Hub after you select your specific goal, class, and subject. They are also highlighted on the main Home dashboard.
How will PDFs be organized?
PDFs are strictly categorized by Board/State, Class/Semester, Subject, and type (e.g., Notes, Formulas, Important/VVI).
Can I search for a PDF?
Yes, you can use the global search bar on the Home page or the dedicated search box inside the Student Hub to find specific PDF resources quickly.

PYQ & Model Paper FAQ

What is PYQ?
PYQ stands for Previous Year Question paper. Practicing these helps you understand the exam pattern and frequently asked concepts.
Where can I find previous year question papers?
You can find them in the dedicated "Question Papers & PYQ" section within the Student Hub, filtered by your specific board or exam.
What are Model Papers and how are they different?
Model Papers are practice sets created based on the latest exam syllabus and format. Unlike PYQs (which are past official exams), Model Papers contain highly probable questions for upcoming exams.

Updates & Scholarship FAQ

What is the Latest Updates section?
It is a central hub for verified, current student opportunities including scholarships, exam notices, government jobs, results, and admit cards.
What is PMS and NSP?
PMS (Post Matric Scholarship) is a state-level scheme (e.g., Bihar PMS) for higher education funding. NSP (National Scholarship Portal) is the central government portal for national-level scholarships.
Where should I apply for a scholarship?
LK SMART STUDIES provides information and official links. You must always verify eligibility and submit your application on the verified official government portal.

Website & PWA FAQ

What is a PWA?
PWA stands for Progressive Web App. It allows you to "install" the LK SMART STUDIES website onto your mobile or desktop device directly from your browser, without needing to visit an app store.
Does PWA require an Android app?
No. PWA technology runs through your web browser. You can simply tap "Add to Home Screen" in your browser menu, and it will function like a native app.
How can I return to Home?
You can return to the Home dashboard at any time by clicking the LK SMART STUDIES logo in the top header, or by using the bottom navigation menu on mobile.
